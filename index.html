I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at Stony Brook University, working on industrial organization, insurance markets, and climate risk. My research uses structural methods to study how insurance markets adapt to environmental change.

My job market paper studies the California homeowners insurance market under Proposition 103 rate regulation, combining CDI rate filing panel data with catastrophe model variation to identify the welfare cost of regulatory friction in the face of growing wildfire exposure.

I am advised by Professor Yiyi Zhou , Professor Mark Montgomery, and Professor Sandro Brusco. I will be on the 2027–2028 academic job market.
