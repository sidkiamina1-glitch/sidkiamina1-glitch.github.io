Featured Work

Job Market Paper
Regulatory Friction and Market Exit in the California Homeowners Insurance Market
I develop a three-stage structural model of insurer pricing, participation, and portfolio adjustment under Proposition 103 rate regulation. Using a panel of CDI rate filings and catastrophe model score instruments, I quantify how regulatory friction interacts with growing wildfire exposure to produce market exit and coverage distortions.
